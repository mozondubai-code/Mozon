Our Story

The Art of the Broast

Mozon Broast began with one obsession: the perfect crunch. We marinate every piece in our secret blend, then pressure-fry it so the outside shatters and the inside stays impossibly juicy — that's what "broasted" really means.

From our kitchen in Al Nahda 2, Dubai, we've grown into a family favourite across the UAE, Saudi Arabia and India — always 100% halal, always made fresh to order, never sitting under a lamp.
