## ⭐ Review engine (ranking + trust)

Ask every happy customer. Steady beats spikes.

**On receipts / bags / table card:**
> Loved your broast? A 10-second Google review means the world 🙏 → *(your review short-link / QR)*

**WhatsApp follow-up after delivery:**
> Thanks for ordering Mozon Broast! 🍗 If it hit the spot, a quick Google review helps us a lot: *(link)*. See you next time!

*Reply to every review — good and bad. Response rate is a ranking signal.* Make a QR in Canva pointing at your Google review link and print it.
