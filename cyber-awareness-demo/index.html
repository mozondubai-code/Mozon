What just happened?
You typed credentials into a page that looked real. Attackers copy the look of Instagram, banks, and college portals to trick you. This demo captured nothing — no password left this page — but a real phishing page would have sent it straight to an attacker.
How to spot a fake login — every time:
  • Check the web address (URL) carefully — look for misspellings like faceb00k or instagrarn.
  • Make sure it starts with https:// and is the official domain.
  • Never log in from a link in an email or SMS — type the address yourself.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) everywhere you can.
  • Never reuse passwords across sites.
  • When in doubt, stop and report it to your teacher / IT team.
