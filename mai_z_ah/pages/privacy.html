Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you place an order, you share your name, phone number and delivery address with us directly through WhatsApp. Your cart, wishlist and theme preference are stored only in your own browser (local storage) — they are not sent to any server.

How we use it

We use your details solely to process and deliver your order and to communicate with you about it. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

WhatsApp

Orders are sent via WhatsApp, which has its own privacy policy. Please review WhatsApp's terms for how they handle messages.

Cookies & storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your cart and preferences. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Your choices

You can clear your saved cart and preferences anytime by clearing your browser data. To request deletion of any order information, contact us on WhatsApp.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us on WhatsApp or via our Contact page.
