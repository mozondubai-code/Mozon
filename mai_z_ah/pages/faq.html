Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse products, tap Add to Cart, adjust quantities, then open your cart and tap Checkout. Fill in your delivery details and tap Place Order on WhatsApp — your order opens as a ready-to-send WhatsApp message. Just hit send!

How is delivery charged?

A small delivery charge is added at checkout. Orders above our free-delivery threshold ship free — you'll see this automatically in your cart total.

What payment methods do you accept?

We confirm your order over WhatsApp and share payment options there (cash on delivery, bank transfer, or QR/UPI where available).

Can I cancel or change my order?

Yes — just message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible and we'll help before your order ships.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends on your location. We'll confirm the estimated time when you place your order on WhatsApp.

Are the products genuine?

Absolutely. Every product is carefully sourced and quality-checked. If you're ever unhappy, message us and we'll make it right.
